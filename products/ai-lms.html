AI-Powered LMS

A learning platform built for engineering teams, where AI does the work of adapting curriculum, assessment and pacing to each learner.

Why we are building it

We have run corporate learning programs, built curricula for immersion training, and taught Java to people changing careers. The recurring failure is not content. it is fit. A fixed syllabus is always wrong for somebody: too slow for the senior engineer, too fast for the person coming from a different stack.

What we are building

A platform that treats curriculum as something generated per learner rather than authored once. Assessment that tells an engineering manager what their team can actually do, and pacing that adapts instead of assuming.

  • Curriculum adapted to the learner's existing stack and level
  • Assessment that maps to real engineering capability
  • Built for technical teams, not general corporate compliance training

Where it stands

This grows directly out of our training practice. The courses and corporate programs that will feed it are running today.

We publish product pages before the product ships on purpose, because it keeps us honest about what exists and what does not. Everything on this page describes work in progress, not a shipping release.
