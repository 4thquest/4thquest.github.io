Services

Planning to rebuild, refactor or replace? We run a thorough as-is assessment and give you a target state with a credible path between them, whether that is a legacy monolith heading toward microservices, or a third-party system you need evaluated honestly before you commit to it.

How we engage

Start small, prove it, then scale

We would rather earn the next phase than sign you to a long one up front. Most work starts in one of three shapes.

01

Assessment

A fixed-scope review of what you have, covering architecture, code, platform or delivery, ending in a written findings document and a roadmap you own.

Typically 2–4 weeks
02

Proof of concept

We build the risky part first, whether that is the integration, the model or the platform spike, so the decision to go further rests on working code rather than estimates.

Typically 4–8 weeks
03

Embedded engineering

We work alongside your team on delivery or platform build-out, and we transfer what we know as we go. The goal is that you do not need us afterwards.

Ongoing, reviewed quarterly
